


  Monetization, given BYOK kills the per-token markup:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Your original model (SPEC doesn't state one explicitly, but the implicit one for any router product is: proxy the call, mark up the token cost) is gone once users plug in their own keys. Alternatives, roughly best-fit first:                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  1. Hybrid BYOK + managed keys — BYOK stays free; users who don't want to manage keys pay you for a hosted/managed key with a markup. You keep the trust-building free tier and still have a paid lane.                                                                                                                                                                  
  2. Flat subscription for the routing service itself — you're selling the decision, not the tokens: routing intelligence, savings dashboard, history, EU-only guarantees. Monthly/seat-based, independent of token volume.                                                                                                                                               
  3. Enterprise/team tier — admin console, budget caps, policy enforcement (force EU-only org-wide), audit logs, usage analytics across a team. This is where the real money usually is for a routing/governance tool — sell to whoever owns the AI spend line, not the individual dev.                                                                                   
  4. Small flat fee per routed decision — a few tenths of a cent per route, positioned explicitly as "cost of routing infrastructure," not a token markup. Transparent, fits your differentiator (you're already showing users the savings math — showing the fee alongside it is consistent).
